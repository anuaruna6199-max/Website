Questions, answered.

How are your pieces designed? ▼

Our design process begins with extensive research into traditional techniques and contemporary aesthetics. We collaborate with artisans to ensure each piece meets our rigorous standards.

What materials do you use? ▼

We use high-quality brass and other non-precious metals with 18k gold plating. Each piece is carefully crafted to ensure durability and beauty while maintaining accessibility and ethical sourcing standards.

Do you offer custom pieces? ▼

Yes, we welcome bespoke commissions. Please reach out through our contact form to discuss your vision.

What is your return and refund policy? ▼

Returns and refunds are not possible. However, for certain pieces, replacement is available if quality issues arise. Please note that an unboxing video is mandatory for any replacement claims.

Our Replacement Policy

Please Note: Returns and refunds are not possible. However, for certain pieces, replacement is available if quality issues arise.

Unboxing Video Requirement

To qualify for replacement, an unboxing video is mandatory. This helps us assess the condition of your piece upon arrival and ensure the authenticity of any claims. When recording your unboxing video:

  • Capture the entire unboxing process
  • Show the piece clearly from all angles
  • Document any visible damage immediately
  • Keep the video for your records

This policy ensures we maintain the highest standards of quality and authenticity across all SEMMAI pieces.
